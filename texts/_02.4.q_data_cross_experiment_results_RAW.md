# Cross-Experiment Summary — Questionnaire (SoA Ratings) Results

All three experiments used the same questions to measure the explicit SoA raitings. This gave us the opportunity to combine them and execute several statistical analysys to explore the role of motion (movement modality) and its influence on the explicit SoA ratings. Analisys are listed below. 

**Descriptives**

Across all three experiments, ratings followed the same broad ordering: highest in the active conditions, lowest in the control condition with the inconsistent condition placed somewhere in the middle. Overall the raitings were more spreadth in exp 1 and numerically more close between the conditions in exp 2 and 3. Mean values are displayed in the table ... The three items did not always agree on their internal ordering, however — in Experiment 1, causation was rated highest overall, followed by prediction and then control; in Experiments 2 and 3, prediction was instead rated numerically highest, with causation and control close together at the bottom. Note that those are the overall raitings, but they higly varried between conditions.

**Analyses**

In order to check the movement modality influence on the SoA raitings a 3 (Question type: caused, controlled, predicted) × 3 (Action Consistency: consistent, inconsistent, control) repeated-measures ANOVA on the raw questionnaire ratings, with Greenhouse–Geisser correction applied to all within-subject terms with more than one degree of freedom was executed with final sample of 138 subjets with experiment as between sibject factor.
