PIPELINE ARCHITECTURE (changed 2026-07-16)
==========================================================================
The two pipelines are no longer coupled through accuracy. Previously questions/pipeline.ipynb read
exp_data's accuracy-excluded subject list and dropped them from its own data too — this dependency
was removed: accuracy can only be computed in exp_data anyway, and low-accuracy subjects naturally
disappear from the final analysis when exp_data's Step 10 merge (left join on exp_data's subject
list) leaves them out. Questions' own final N is therefore now larger (50) than it used to be (42)
— that's expected, not a regression; it only reflects Likert exclusion, same as always.

The one thing that still flows between the two pipelines is the Likert exclusion itself — a
whole-subject judgment (not attending to the rating questions is a proxy for not attending to the
task), so it has to remove the RT data too, not just the ratings. This is now a direct file:
questions/pipeline.ipynb Step 03 writes `03_likert_excluded_subjects.txt` (plain comma-separated
IDs); exp_data Step 03 reads it directly. No more recomputing the exclusion formula on the exp_data
side, and nothing hardcoded.

ANCOVA COVARIATE
================
09_initiation_time.xlsx: mean `initiation_time` (SlideXeroA.RT) per subject, computed only across
action trials (consistent + inconsistent) — control trials use XeroDur (an arbitrary experimenter
delay, not a real behavioral measure) and are excluded from this average entirely, since there's no
comparable "temporal prediction" construct to represent for them. Merged into
10_questions_data_added.xlsx as `mean_initiation_time`. Any ANCOVA using this covariate should
itself be restricted to the 4 action-condition cells (cong_cons, cong_incons, incong_cons,
incong_incons) — do not include the *_ctrl columns in that analysis.
